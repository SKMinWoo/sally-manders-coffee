Sally Mander’s, five ways

One shop, one salamander, five looks. Each page has the storefront and, behind the header switch, the back office for the same simulated day. Sally's drawing is the same in all five; only the inks and type change. Her settled mark is on the logo sheet.

What works

The Storefront / Behind the scenes switch on every page. Add ?view=back to a link to open straight on the back office.

What is stubbed

Ordering, the menu tabs, the stamp card and every other link. The numbers are one seeded day from the simulator (258 orders, $1,758.75), not live.

Type is separable

Each theme sets the wordmark in its own face. The logo sheet's Caprasimo and Archivo could stay the brand type under any of them.
