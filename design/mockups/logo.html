Sally Mander's · logo sheet · a simulated coffee shop in Austin, Texas

Sally is a Barton Springs salamander: she lives in the springs of one Austin creek and nowhere else. She is drawn in profile, as the real animal is usually seen: a long flat head, a small gold-ringed eye, three feathered red gills a side, a freckled mauve body and a long tail. Here she lives in a mug, her chin on the rim, facing her name. The name is set in Caprasimo, a soft, heavy display serif, over a line of condensed Archivo.
